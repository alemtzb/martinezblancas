Welcome!

The Martínez Blancas Lab focuses on understanding the role of species interactions and abiotic factors in maintaining diversity in plant communities. We work at the intersection of population and community ecology and use quantitative methods coupled with field data to develop tools for understanding ecological processes. We are also committed to grassland conservation.
