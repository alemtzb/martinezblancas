Welcome!

The Martínez Blancas lab focuses on understanding the role of species interactions and abiotic factors on diversity maintenance in plant communities. We work at the intersection of population and community ecology and use quantitative methods coupled with field data to develop tools to understand ecological processes. We are also committed to grassland conservation.
